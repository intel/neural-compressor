neural_compressor.torch.algorithms.fake_quant.functional

Hot-path Quantize-Dequantize (QDQ) kernels, STE autograd wrapper, and backend registry.

Design invariants for training hot paths: 1. Zero bit-packing / unpacking or repeat_interleave expansion during QDQ. 2. Per-device lookup-table caching (zero CPU-to-device H2D transfers per forward). 3. Zero host-device synchronization (no float(tensor) / .item() branches),

enabling torch.compile(..., fullgraph=True) with zero graph breaks.

  1. Zero saved tensors in StraightThroughFakeQuant backward.

  2. Exact bit-level numerical parity with ModelOpt’s CUDA MXFP4, NVFP4, and FP8 E4M3 kernels.
