class neural_compressor.torch.algorithms.fake_quant.plugins.peft.QuantLoraLinear[source]

Fake-quantized PEFT LoraLinear supporting both deployment-aligned Path-B and Path-A.

  • quant_order="lora_then_quant" (Path B, default): y = Q_out(F.linear(Q_in(x), Q_w(W_base + sum_i ((s_i * B_i) @ A_i)), b)) Matches NVIDIA ModelOpt’s _QuantLoraLinear and Megatron-Bridge’s MergedLoRALinear bit-for-bit, ensuring zero train-to-export quantization mismatch once adapters are merged.

  • quant_order="quant_then_lora" (Path A): y = Q_out(F.linear(Q_in(x), Q_w(W_base), b)) + sum_i s_i * B_i(A_i(dropout_i(x))) Matches standard PEFT wrapping a QuantLinear base layer.
