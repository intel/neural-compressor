Replace modules with fake-quant subclasses and optionally calibrate.

Parameters:

  • model – Float model, modified in place.

  • configs_mapping – {(module_name, op_type): ModuleQuantConfig} from FakeQuantConfig.to_config_mapping().

  • mode – PREPARE inserts quantizers only; QUANTIZE also calibrates static quantizers when run_fn is given; CONVERT finalizes calibration without replacing the fake-quant modules.

  • run_fn – Calibration forward loop, called as run_fn(model, *run_args).

  • run_args – Positional arguments for run_fn.
