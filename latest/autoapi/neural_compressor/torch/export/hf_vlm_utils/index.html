neural_compressor.torch.export.hf_vlm_utils

Streaming safetensors shard writer and multimodal VLM text-tower surgery utilities.

Supports exporting large (27B+) text-tower QAT/QAD models back into full multimodal HuggingFace checkpoints (e.g. Qwen3_5ForConditionalGeneration with model.visual.* and mtp.*) while keeping peak host memory bounded by <= 2 * max_shard_size.

class neural_compressor.torch.export.hf_vlm_utils.StreamingSafetensorsWriter(output_dir: str | pathlib.Path, max_shard_size: str | int = '5GB')[source]

Bounded-memory sharded safetensors writer.

Tensors are accumulated up to max_shard_size bytes and flushed immediately to temporary shard files; finalize() renames them to HuggingFace canonical shard names (model.safetensors or model-XXXXX-of-YYYYY.safetensors) and emits model.safetensors.index.json when num_shards > 1.
